# Play Console — pre-filled questionnaire answers

Two forms are required before Play will let you publish: **Data Safety**
and **Content Rating**. Both are simple for this app because we collect
nothing and the content is non-violent strategy. Use this as a copy-paste
reference while walking through the Console UI.

---

## Data Safety form

Found at: *App content → Data safety → Manage*.

### Section 1 — Data collection and security

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? | **No** |
| Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit? | N/A (no data collected) — but if forced to pick: **Yes** |
| Do you provide a way for users to request that their data be deleted? | N/A (no data collected) — but if forced to pick: **No** (there is no data to delete; uninstall clears the local sandbox) |

Because the first answer is **No**, Play skips the rest of the form — no
data type sub-questions, no third-party sharing matrix.

### Section 2 — Final declaration

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| The information provided here is complete and accurate. | ✓ |
| You understand that you are responsible for keeping it up to date. | ✓ |

## Target audience and content

Found at: *App content → Target audience and content*.

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Target age groups | **13–15, 16–17, 18+** (recommended — game complexity is realistic, not aimed at small children, but contains nothing unsuitable for them) |
| Does the app appeal to children? | **No** (avoids the stricter "Designed for Families" review track) |
| Will you publish ads in the app? | **No** |

If you pick *any* age range that includes under-13, Play will ask
additional Designed-for-Families questions and apply COPPA-style
restrictions. Keep it 13+ to skip that path.

## Summary

Every single content/safety question is **No** for this app. The whole
flow should take ~15 minutes in Console once you've copied these answers.
The only judgement call is the **target age range** — going 13+ rather
than 6+ saves you the Designed-for-Families review hassle without
limiting your audience meaningfully (a 10-year-old who wants to play
this can still install it; they just need a parent's account or a
Family Link setup, which is true for most strategy games anyway).
