Terre Vivante — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-22

Terre Vivante ("the app", "we") does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

What the app does NOT do

  • The app does not request network permissions — it has no INTERNET permission in its Android manifest and cannot make outbound connections of any kind. Game logic runs entirely on your device.
  • The app does not request storage permissions beyond the per-app sandbox Android grants every app for its own save files (game progress, preferences). That sandbox is private to this app and is removed automatically when you uninstall.
  • The app does not collect or share analytics, crash reports, advertising identifiers, location, contacts, microphone, camera, or any other device data.
  • The app does not include third-party SDKs for analytics, ads, attribution, or any other data processing.

What the app DOES store, locally only

Game progress (current run state, settings, completed runs history) is saved to the app's private storage on your device. This data:

  • Never leaves your device.
  • Is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its app data through Android's Settings → Apps screen.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences. It is not directed at children under 13, but contains no content unsuitable for them and collects no data from any user.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes the above (e.g. adds an online leaderboard or cloud save), this policy will be updated and the app's Play Store listing will reflect the new data practices. Until you see such an update, you can assume the policy on this page is accurate.
